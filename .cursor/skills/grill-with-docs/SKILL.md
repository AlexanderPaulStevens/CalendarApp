  Stress-test a plan or design and record the outcome as glossary terms and
  ADRs while the interview proceeds. Use when the user wants to grill a plan
  against the project's language and documented decisions.
---

Run a grilling session and apply the domain-modeling skill in the same session.

1. Read and follow the `grilling` skill: one question at a time, each with a recommended answer. Do not update docs from grilling alone.
2. Read and follow the `domain-modeling` skill: challenge terms, sharpen language, and update `CONTEXT.md` and ADRs as decisions crystallise.
